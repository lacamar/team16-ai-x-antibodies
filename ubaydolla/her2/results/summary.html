The one-paragraph argument

Trastuzumab is already a clinically optimised binder at KD 0.095 nM, and the optimisation track forbids staying near it: a design needs >2 point mutations from every one of the 525,063 provided antibodies. Our first finding was that this rule and the binding axis pull against each other — RFD4 satisfies novelty by jumping 6–8 mutations from a tuned loop, and none of its 73 novelty-passing designs was predicted to bind as well (best ≈15 nM, ~150× worse). So we stopped sampling and searched instead for the least-damaging novelty-compliant perturbation: because the provided library covers only 5.25×105 of 1013 possible 10-mers, compliant sequences exist at just 4 mutations from trastuzumab. We enumerated that shell exactly, then required independent methods to agree before promoting anything.

Lead: TZ-OPT-01 · WGSQGFYEME

Four mutations — G101S,D102Q,A106E,D108E — on an otherwise byte-identical trastuzumab scFv (242 aa).

  • Axis 1 (binding). Above the trastuzumab control on 4 of 5 independent scorers, including the SPR-trained RandomForest (0.322 vs 0.127 measured) and StaB-ddG (-0.173 kcal/mol, stabilising). It is not the ipTM leader, which is the point: we rank on agreement, not on any single maximum.
  • Axis 2 (epitope). Retains all 4/4 conditioned HER2 hotspot contacts and 0.818 Jaccard overlap with the residues trastuzumab itself contacts in the same folding run — so trastuzumab should still compete it off, which is what the assay measures.
  • Axis 3 (stability). The framework, the whole VL, the VH/VL packing and both disulfides are unchanged, and AntiFold framework perplexity is flat across all 41 structures (1.92–2.10), confirming the framework is genuinely unperturbed. On top of that it deletes trastuzumab's own Asp102–Gly103 isomerisation motif and is markedly less aggregation-prone (Δagg -1.06).

Judgment calls worth defending

We rank on unanimity, because our scorers disagree. On this 40-design shortlist StaB-ddG anti-correlates with the SPR model (ρ=−0.405, p=0.009) and with Boltz-2 ipTM (ρ=−0.338, p=0.033), while ipTM and the SPR model agree with each other (ρ=+0.343). Two pairs agree, two anti-correlate. Averaging percentiles would have hidden that, so the primary key is instead how many independent scorers beat the control. Earlier in the day a single-model argmax produced a candidate "1.6 log units tighter" that collapsed under cross-checking — that is scorer-adversarial extrapolation, and it is why nothing here is promoted on one model.
We dropped pLDDT, and we dropped the designs most likely to bind. Binder pLDDT correlates ρ=−0.013 with our empirical affinity model across 100 designs — it is noise for this task, so it is not a ranking key. Separately, the two best binders we found anywhere (WGSSGFYAMD, WGYSGFYAMD: top-2 on the SPR model and 3rd/5th of 100 on ipTM) carry only 2 CDRH3 mutations and therefore fail the track rule. They are in results/rejected_designs.csv as rows with reasons, not silently dropped, and we re-ran them through StaB-ddG purely to calibrate its sign convention.
Gates re-verified from scratch, not inherited. A filter applied by the generator is no evidence the shipped molecule passes, so verify_gates.py recomputes the exact minimum Hamming distance from every shipped CDRH3 to all 525,063 provided CDRH3s by enumerating radius 0/1/2 — exact, not sampled. 40/40 designs pass; all are at distance exactly 3 (so >2 mutations from every provided antibody) and 4–5 from trastuzumab, i.e. ≤60% CDRH3 identity, which clears the <70% rule under either reading. scFv 242 aa ≤ 250, no unpaired cysteines. The control correctly fails, which is the check working.
What we could not verify. abguard does not exist on this VM — not on PATH, not in pip, not on PyPI, not inside any Apheris image, and absent from a recursive search of the hackathon bucket. The checks above are our reimplementation of its four documented rules. The one we cannot reproduce is immunum numbering confidence ≥0.1, which needs ANARCI/ANARCII; neither installs here, and AntiFold's bundled ANARCII failed to number our Boltz-2 output chains. Every design is a 242-aa scFv on an unmodified clinical framework so the risk is low, but it is unverified and we say so rather than implying a clean pass.
Thermostability is argued by construction, not predicted. No Tm predictor was available on either lane — Aggrescan3D, SoluProt, NetSolP and ProteinMPNN-soluble are all absent, and StaB-ddG is a binding ddG, not a folding one. We did not substitute a proxy and call it stability. The claim is narrower and checkable: the framework is identical to the clinical molecule, so Fv Tm is near-constant; what we changed is a surface loop, so what we score is the loop's own aggregation, charge and chemical-liability contribution, deterministically. Honest caveat: AntiFold CDRH3 perplexity is slightly worse than trastuzumab's own loop for 37 of 40 designs, so loop–backbone compatibility is the one axis-3 signal that does not favour us.

How it was produced

  • Generation (lane A, H100). RFD4-Proteina her2-novel-2026-11-09, 100 replicates, seed 1, no sequence_tsr_k — the setting with the best novelty pass rate (86%) and lowest interface error (6.8 Å). 11.4 min. Used to characterise the novelty/affinity tension; not the source of the shipped list.
  • Shell enumeration (CPU). The ridge affinity model on one-hot CDRH3 is additive, so score(s) = intercept + Σp W[p,sp] and the 4–5-mutation shell (2,231,712 sequences) is searched exactly, with no sampling.
  • Empirical scorers. HistGradientBoosting on 367,044 clonotype-split FACS labels (test AUC 0.991) and a RandomForest/ridge on 180 quantified SPR KDs (CV ρ=0.72). Externally validated on data never seen: Shanehsazzadeh 198 designs ρ=0.297 (p=2.1e-5), SPR 187 KDs ρ=0.353 (p=7.2e-7).
  • Folding (lane A). Boltz-2 co-fold, templated on the trastuzumab–HER2 complex (1N8Z Fv + domain IV), seed 1, with trastuzumab folded in the same batch as the control. 41 structures in 5.6 min.
  • Second opinions (lane B, Nebius, no GPU). StaB-ddG binding ddG on 42 variants (op-79006e6f713984a7), ESM-2 650M pseudo-likelihood (op-07b3495e1f4d104a), AntiFold inverse-folding perplexity on 41 Fv backbones (op-441067223204e140). Ran concurrently with lane A.
