# Pitch — 5 slides

1. **User & problem** — who needs this, the real problem, why it matters (User fit).
2. **Funnel** — generate → score → filter, with counts per stage and the methods used (Execution).
3. **Top-10 table** — the shortlisted designs with their scores and provenance (rendered, not typed).
4. **Three key decisions** — for each: what we chose, what we rejected, and why (Judgment). Show a scorer-control / baseline row as validity evidence.
5. **Next steps** — credible path to real use for the molecule or the method (Path to value).

Keep it to 5 minutes. The numbers come from `docs/report.md`; the decisions from `DECISIONS.md`.
